# Codex: the owner's decisions

Part of [codex](codex.md) and [codex-limits](codex-limits.md): what the owner allowed for a
consult, with dates and words. The checks before the call are on the limits page.

- **The standing yes**, 16 November. With the limits page's checks passed, a session may start a
  consult on its own judgement, for code in the owner's own repositories, without a question
  in chat; the report names the model and the repository.
- **Unattended**, 17 November, two yeses in the question dialog: "May Claude start Codex
  without you watching, for read-only second opinions on our own work?", then "Claude may
  start a consult that runs without your clicks, with full access, on your own repositories
  only, after the scan, ignored-files and start checks." Only the second names full access.
- **Never pointed at.** Secrets, keys and tokens; customer data; personal data; files outside
  the repository, beyond Codex's own start-up reading.
- **Ask first.** Anything else: a repository that is not the owner's, a client's private
  material, data exports, logs with real users in them. The question names the provider, the
  model and what would be sent, by path, and why. No yes, no consult.
- These answer findings of GitHub's security review: the owner chose the standing yes twice
  on 16 November over a confirmation before every consult, the second time knowing that the
  sandbox does not fence reading and that the session decides alone. Loosening either yes is
  the owner's decision.
