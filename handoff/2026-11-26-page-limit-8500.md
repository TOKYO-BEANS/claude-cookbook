# 26 November 2026: a page may cost 8,500 bytes as a Read result (was 4,000)

**Why.** The owner's answer. Asked with the question dialog in the chat "Weekly fixer: one
workaround becomes a fix; stale memory pruned" on 26 November 2026: "Should the page limit
(PAGE_READ_LIMIT in scripts/check.mjs) be raised?" The options were 8,000, 6,000, keep 4,000 and
not now; the owner typed "Raise to 8500". 35 pages sat within 150 bytes of 4,000, so each new
rule meant cutting words by hand. This loosens a check, so it needed that yes.

**What changed.**
- The cut first. context-mode cut any tool result over 8,192 bytes, below the new limit, so
  machine-setup #53 (merged before this) set `CTX_REPLACE_TRIGGER_BYTES` to 8600. Measured there
  on Claude Code 2.1.332: for a Read the hook counts the file's raw bytes, not the numbered text;
  a page at read cost 8,500 has at most 8,491 raw bytes; a page at 8,500 with 1,900-byte lines
  (8,428 raw bytes) was cut at 8,192 and came back whole at 8,600.
- `scripts/check.mjs`: `PAGE_READ_LIMIT` is 8500, with its comment. `readCost` (bytes plus 9 a
  line) and `SKILL_LIMIT` (14,000) are unchanged.
- `scripts/check.test.mjs`: the one literal "over 4000" is "over 8500". The helper `filled`
  now measures without carriage returns, as the size check does (the low finding of the AI
  review of #262): on a CRLF checkout `fullSkill` and `fullPage` came out short of the limit.
- The texts that cite the numbers: `recipes/context-mode.md` (the cut, its history, a new point
  on why a page fits one read, and the scratchpad advice no longer tied to 3,700 bytes),
  `options/keep-healthy.md`, `recipes/update-check.md`, the `stack-updates` skill, the `reader`
  and `reader-graph` profiles, a comment in `updates.mjs` and one in its test, `README.md`.
- `PAGE_BYTES` (3,500) in the stack-updates script and its test "under 4,096" stay: a report
  page is sized for context-mode's default cut, so it also fits in a session or on a machine
  without the setting. Only the comments that cited 8,192 changed.
- `HANDOFF.md` still says 4,000 and 4,096: that file is closed and is the record of its day.

**Verification.** `node scripts/check.mjs --room`: `Cookbook check passed.`, and no page is
within 150 bytes of the limit any more. The helper on a page with CRLF line ends, before: the
filled page costs 8,489 of 8,500 and one byte more gives no size error; after: 8,500 of 8,500,
and one byte more gives `recipes/seo.md: 8501 bytes as a Read result, over 8500: split the
page`. The full suite in a Depot sandbox for the head before the push. The cut: three fresh
plan-token `claude -p` runs, recorded in machine-setup's handoff entry of the same day.

**Outstanding.** None. A machine without the setting cuts at 4,096: there a page over 4,096 raw
bytes is read in ranges, as `recipes/context-mode.md` now says. Pages may grow, but a page that
passes 8,500 is still split.

**Dead ends.** A cut of exactly 8,500: enough by the arithmetic, but it leaves nothing for a
hook version that counts the result with a short header; 8,600 costs at most 408 bytes more per
tool result. Raising `PAGE_BYTES` with the limit: it would break the update report wherever the
default cut applies.
