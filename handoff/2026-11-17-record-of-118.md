# 17 November 2026: the record of #118, and merges that queue up

**Why.** #118 (`handoff/2026-11-17-record-of-115.md`) merged within two minutes of #116 and
#117 from two other chats, and the merges cancelled each other's CI. This is its record, and
the change that stops it happening again.

## What changed here

- `recipes/ci-releases.md`: "do not merge back to back" is replaced by what enforces it:
  `merge-when-green.sh` waits for the default branch's CI before it merges
  (TOKYO-BEANS/release-maintenance#30), and a hand merge checks first.

## What cancelled the runs

The CI workflows the standard writes (`.depot/workflows/ci.yml`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`)
set `concurrency: ${{ github.workflow }}-${{ github.ref }}` with `cancel-in-progress: true`
(Stack Atlas `standard/apply.mjs`). A push to `main` cancels the run of the `main` commit
before it, in Depot CI and in the GitHub workflow alike. The CI files were not changed: that is
a new standard version and a CI change for the owner. The wait in the merge script removes the
collision for every merge made through it.
