## DMARC for `nofix.dev` (owner's yes, question dialog, 18 November 2026)

The domain sends no mail, but it had no DMARC record and its SPF ends `~all`, so mail claiming
to come from `nofix.dev` was not refused. Added through the Cloudflare API (key
`cloudflare-wrangler`): TXT `_dmarc.nofix.dev` = `v=DMARC1; p=reject`, record id
`5605b42423844be6bb549b349c1b0267`, with a comment saying why. Checked: 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8
both return it, and `https://nofix.dev/` and `/privacy` still answer 200. To undo it, delete
that one record. The `in.nofix.dev` inbox is not affected: DMARC covers sending, not receiving.
`recipes/cloudflare.md` now says so: mail sent from `nofix.dev` is refused until the record
changes.
